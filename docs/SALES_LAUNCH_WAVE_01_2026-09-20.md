## Decision boundary
No unsolicited cold email is used for Wave 01. No automated cold calling is used.
Wave 01 uses personalized postal direct mail only, clearly identifiable as advertising, with stop-on-objection handling.
Reason: German UWG §7 requires prior express consent for advertising by electronic mail; B2B telephone advertising requires at least presumed consent based on specific objective circumstances. Postal advertising is generally the lower-risk route if the recipient has not objected.
