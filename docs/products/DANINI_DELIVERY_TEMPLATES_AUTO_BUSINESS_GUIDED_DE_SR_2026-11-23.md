# DANINI — kvalitet isporuke: AutoDanini / Business / Guided Setup
**23.11.2026 · INTERNAL TEMPLATE + FICTIONAL SAMPLES · NOT A REAL CUSTOMER CASE · NOT FOR AUTOMATED SALE**
Purpose: prevent a paid order from receiving raw AI output or an unsourced generic summary. Every commissioned deliverable must be attached to an approved statement of work (SOW), assigned an owner and reviewed by a human.

## Universal delivery dossier — required in every language
Document ID: [reference] · Product SKU: [selected and validated] · Edition [vX] · Customer/contract category: [B2C/B2B] · Date/time and timezone [ISO] · Scope confirmation [link/reference] · Responsible reviewer [role] · Sources [full URLs + exact dates + who provided] · Price/invoice ref [approved separately] · Retention class [approved].
**Source classes:** CLIENT CLAIM / PROVIDED DOCUMENT (authenticity NOT verified) / INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED SOURCE (what/how/date) / INFERENCE OR ESTIMATE / UNKNOWN. Never label source-class 2 as independently verified.
**Quality states:** DRAFT / NEEDS_INFO / QA_REVIEW / APPROVED_FOR_DELIVERY / DELIVERED / REVISION_DUE / CLOSED. Client payment/capture is a separate factual status.
**Delivery checklist:** no invented facts, privacy and third-party rights, correct localization, all links and calculations, explicit exclusions, date/edition, customer action list, secure access, acceptance and correction/complaint contact. STOP if the report promises a result beyond SOW.

| Unfallfrei / Bez sudara | Seller's listing text, dated [sample] | CLIENT CLAIM; **not checked** | Request written confirmation; independent inspection |
| Wartung vollständig / Servis uredan | No actual invoices | UNKNOWN | Request dated bills / HU history |
| MwSt. ausweisbar | No seller invoice | UNKNOWN | Request preliminary invoice; consult qualified tax advisor |
| EU Präferenzursprung | No origin document | UNKNOWN | Ask about appropriate proof and competent customs advice |

**German seller message, DRAFT requiring customer approval:**
"Guten Tag, bitte bestätigen Sie den rechtlichen Verkäufer und den Umsatzsteuerstatus und senden Sie vor einer Anzahlung, soweit vorhanden, die Zulassungsbescheinigungen I/II, CoC und die letzten HU-/Servicenachweise zur Einsicht. ## BIZ-01 — Geschäftsanfragen-Check / Provera poslovnog upita
**Deliverables:** one documented workflow AS-IS/TO-BE, gap matrix, anonymized sample response, intake fields, human approval checkpoint and one revision.
**FICTIONAL sample:** a hypothetical small supplier receives German request for a part with no quantity, drawing version or shipping date.
| AS-IS step | Identified gap | Proposed improvement | Owner and verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email received | Volume/version missing | Single intake field list | Authorized customer staff to verify |
| Drafting response | Prices could be invented | Ask only 3 factual missing-field questions | Owner approves text |
| CRM record | No status or case reference | Stable reference, state OPEN/WAITING/APPROVED | D1/CRM access only after SOW and lawful basis |
| Reply | Risk of automatic third-party contact | Manual approval and send by customer | No DANINI external message without permission |
**DE response draft:**
"Vielen Dank für Ihre Anfrage. Für eine prüfbare Angebotserstellung benötigen wir bitte die gewünschte Menge, den aktuellen Zeichnungsstand und den gewünschten Liefertermin. Nach Eingang dieser Angaben können wir den Umfang prüfen. Dies ist noch kein verbindliches Angebot."
**SR explanation:** Hvala na upitu; tražimo količinu, verziju crteža i rok. Ništa nije prihvaćena ponuda. Ovo je primer, ne stvarni kupac.
**Acceptance:** all inputs captured, no invented numbers, draft in idiomatic DE, approval evidence, client can reproduce the five steps, handoff and retention documented.

**Delivered output:** (1) idea and customer problem, (2) 3 dated signals or explicit gaps, (3) 3 competitors/alternatives and sources, (4) one testable offer with exclusions, (5) one DE/SR landing paragraph, (6) consent-safe enquiry handling, (7) simple cost/time/revenue *hypotheses* and 30-day validation checklist, (8) stop criteria, (9) annotated human review and one revision.
**FICTIONAL example:** "remote bilingual admin help for small firms"; no real customer, paid pilot, income or demand represented. Source placeholders MUST be replaced with actual URLs/dates, else NEEDS_INFO.
**Acceptance:** customer has a reproducible proposal, one evidence-checkable test, explicit next 3 actions, editable text and clear disclaimers. No business registration, tax, legal or buyer guarantee.

## Customer acceptance and satisfaction safeguards
1. Before sale send a short DE/SR scope/acceptance overview; do not promise a turnaround without capacity.
2. When delivering provide source/caveat summary, editable or PDF output and correction contact; client sees exactly what was promised.
3. Classify feedback as CLARITY, FACTUAL_ERROR, INCOMPLETE_DELIVERY, TECHNICAL_DOWNLOAD, PRIVACY, PRICING, LEGAL, OTHER. These are operational categories, not analytics already implemented.
4. Customer may report defects with reference; human checks evidence and offers lawful cure, replacement, cancellation/withdrawal or refund per contract/statute.
5. Track median correction time, acceptance disputes, verified delivery success, feedback response count and factual errors with denominators; never invent satisfaction ratings.
6. Any supported claim changed after sale gets an errata entry with edition/hash, impact assessment and lawful remediation for prior buyers.
