## Arithmetic check, NOT a claim of savings
Table totals are arithmetically 12,605+550+0+2,710+820=16,685 EUR and 15,000+650+1,956+3,600+1,350=22,556 EUR; difference 5,871 EUR. However baseline vehicle price, eligibility, customs, transport and service inputs differ. The comparison **does not establish recoverable savings**. The 15,000 EUR gross divided by 1.19 is approximately 12,605 EUR net; not every buyer can get that net price or a refund.
